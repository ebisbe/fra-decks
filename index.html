Reality Fracture — 7 sealed decks

Roc + Uri + Enric · 236 cards · 23 spells + 17 lands each · lists are disjoint, all 7 can be sleeved at once · 18 Nov 2026

WUUBBRRGGWBGUR

Sideboard cards are kept out of the main decks. FRA has a cycle of five narrow colour-hosers — Refute Destiny, Precise Redaction, Terminal Criticism, Essence Burn and Flourishing Grapple. Each one only punishes a single colour pair, so they are in the sideboard plans at the bottom of each deck, never in the 40.
